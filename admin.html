Change this before handing the app to a customer
What this console is, honestly. There is no backend behind this app, so nothing here can police a customer's device. The passcode keeps the panel out of a curious user's way, the keys are checksum-verified rather than cryptographically signed, and the ledger records what this device issued. Treat it as a licence desk, not a DRM system.
